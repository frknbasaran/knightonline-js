Item Id Structure:

Example Item Shard: 1 1121 00 01

1 1121 00 00 -> without prop
1 1121 00 10 -> dex for shard, health for ii, str for raptor
1 1121 00 20 -> resistance to flame
1 1121 00 30 -> reduce
1 1121 00 40 -> primary effect (posion for shard, lightning for ii)
1 1121 00 50 -> regular item
1 1121 00 60 -> magic item
1 1121 00 70 -> magic item + str for rogue, health for warrior etc.
1 1121 00 80 -> magic item + health for rogue
1 1121 00 90 -> magic item + int for rogue
1 1121 01 00 -> magic item + resistance to glacier
1 1121 01 10 -> magic item -> resistance to magic
1 1121 02 10 -> rate item -> mp bonus

if, 1121 is 1131, it means exceptional item
